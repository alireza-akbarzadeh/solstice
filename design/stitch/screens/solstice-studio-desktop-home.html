Guide & Founder

Elena Vance

“Yoga is not about touching your toes; it is about what you discover on the way down.”

Elena began her study along the banks of the Ganges in Rishikesh before deepening her craft through somatic physiology and therapeutic trauma work in Zurich. Solstice was conceived as an antidote to performance-driven, athletic yoga.

Her methodology pairs classical alignment with soft neurological inquiry. Every cue invites you to cultivate intimacy with sensation, unravel habitual holding patterns, and return home to an unhurried mind.
