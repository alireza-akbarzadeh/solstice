## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, stillness, and tactile elegance of a secluded architectural wellness retreat. Crafted for an intimate, instructor-led digital sanctuary, the visual expression bridges fine-art editorial publishing (reminiscent of *Kinfolk* and slow living journals) with modern, frictionless interaction design.

### Design Principles
- **Mindful Stillness:** Reject visual anxiety, gamified clutter, aggressive progress bars, and hyper-saturated urgency. Every screen behaves like a quiet room made of raw plaster, bleached stone, and natural linen.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast, classical serif typography paired with expansive whitespace guides the practitioner naturally, allowing breath and mental pause between interface elements.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Surface treatments reject plastic gloss and harsh dropshadows. Depth is articulated through delicate, linen-tinted tonal sheets and whisper-thin hairline contours.
- **Cinematic Framing:** Video sessions and photography take center stage within soft architectural crops, treating human movement with gallery-level reverence.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from organic pigments, sun-baked clay, unbleached fibers, and mineral stones.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base (`#FDFBF7`):** Warm Ivory. The serene, unhurried foundation for full-bleed viewport backgrounds.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#F5F2EB`):** Soft Cream Linen. Used for sub-sections, grouped content wells, and drawer backdrops.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#FAF8F4`):** Natural Linen. The primary card and elevated container fill, providing quiet contrast over `#F5F2EB` or `#FDFBF7`.
- **Surface Sunken / Stone (`#EAE5DC`):** Sand Stone. Applied to search fields, resting audio players, and subtle inset states.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5DFD5`):** Whisper-soft structural border tint for tactile, razor-thin delineation.

### Typography & Content Tokens
- **Text Primary (`#22201E`):** Deep Mineral Charcoal. Grounded, legible, and softer than pure black.
- **Text Secondary (`#6E675F`):** Warm Muted Taupe. Intended for subtitles, durations, equipment tags, and contemplative notes.
- **Text Tertiary (`#9A9187`):** Light Earth. Used for metadata timestamps, deactivated steps, and subtle captions.

### Accents & Semantic States
- **Botanical Sage (`#536350`):** Primary brand accent. Guides active yoga practice tags, completed markers, audio wave active states, and calm affirmations.
- **Warm Clay Gold (`#BFA37C`):** Secondary accent. Celebrates dedication rituals, series completions, and sanctuary membership highlights.
- **Terracotta Mist (`#A35848`):** Restrained warning or gentle cancellation alert; quiet and non-punitive.
- **Deep Sage Success (`#4C6B52`):** Grounded confirmation for saved sessions and completed flows.

## Typography

The type system blends the contemplative, historical prestige of **Playfair Display** with the ergonomic, highly legible modern geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in Playfair Display. Large display headlines can use gentle italicized words for sensory emphasis (e.g., *breathe*, *flow*, *stillness*). Maintain generous line heights to prevent serif ascenders from colliding.
- **Uppercase Labels:** When using `label-lg` or `label-md` for practice categories, chapter labels, or status indicators, apply `text-transform: uppercase` paired with the defined wide letter spacing to preserve an airy, editorial signature.
- **Reading Length:** Limit body copy measures to 60–68 characters per line to evoke literary ease and prevent visual fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts rely on an asymmetrical, airy rhythm modeled after architectural magazines and art monographs.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1320px, `margin: 3rem`, and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Editorial text layouts leverage off-center 7-column or 8-column spans to afford generous negative space for breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Practice sequences, meditation passages, and video players are afforded extended vertical rhythm (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`), refusing dense grouping.
- Padding inside cards and modular blocks utilizes `space-md` to `space-lg`, guaranteeing that imagery and titles never collide with container perimeters.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through tonal stacking and ambient warmth rather than synthetic drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Palette
- **Resting Surface:** Crisp 1px hairline border in `#E5DFD5` paired with zero elevation for a clean architectural plane.
- **Elevated Canvas (Floating Modals, Class Trays, Audio Miniplex):**
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(34, 32, 30, 0.05), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(34, 32, 30, 0.03)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(229, 223, 213, 0.8)`
- **Interactive Hover:** Elements rise subtly with `translateY(-2px)` accompanied by an ambient bloom: `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(83, 99, 80, 0.08)`.

### Translucency & Frosted Linen
Sticky headers, audio player docks, and navigation overlays utilize background blur:
`background: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px);` with a subtle bottom hairline border in `#E5DFD5`.

## Shapes

The design system employs refined, architectural roundedness rather than bubbly or toy-like pills.

### Geometry Standards
- **Containers & Media (`rounded-lg` / 12px):** Video thumbnails, session cards, modal sheets, and journal wrappers use soft architectural corners that reference dressed stone blocks.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Form fields, action buttons, and segmented pickers maintain an 8px radius to preserve deliberate structure.
- **Tag Badges (`rounded-sm` / 4px to 6px):** Small practice tags (e.g., *Vinyasa*, *Pranayama*) use subtle softness without collapsing into total oval capsules, sustaining the high-fashion editorial tone.
- **Media Insets:** Yoga photography and postures use gentle 12px radii or full arch masks on featured hero portraits.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Grounding Botanical):** Background in Botanical Sage (`#536350`), typography in Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`), `label-lg`, height 48px, horizontal padding 28px, border-radius 8px. Hover shifts softly to `#445241` with gentle easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`).
- **Secondary Action (Warm Linen Outline):** Transparent surface with 1px border in Deep Mineral Charcoal (`#22201E`), text in `#22201E`. Hover fills with Sand Stone (`#EAE5DC`).
- **Tertiary / Text Button:** Deep Mineral Charcoal text with a permanent or animated 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline.

### Session & Class Cards
- **Structure:** Surface Natural Linen (`#FAF8F4`), 1px perimeter border (`#E5DFD5`), 12px radius, zero drop shadow.
- **Media Frame:** 16:9 or 4:5 aspect ratio image at the top with a 10px inner radius. Video duration pill placed top right with frosted backdrop (`rgba(34, 32, 30, 0.65)` and white typography).
- **Meta Content:** Practice type in `label-md` Botanical Sage, session title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, and instructor / intensity row in `body-sm` Warm Muted Taupe.

### Input Fields
- **Resting State:** Fill in Sand Stone (`#EAE5DC`), border 1px solid transparent, height 48px, padding horizontal 16px, border-radius 8px. Typography in `body-md` Deep Mineral Charcoal. Placeholder in Light Earth (`#9A9187`).
- **Focused State:** Background shifts to Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`), border becomes 1px solid Botanical Sage (`#536350`), accompanied by a delicate 2px outer aura in `rgba(83, 99, 80, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Resting Filter:** Background `#FAF8F4`, border 1px solid `#E5DFD5`, text Warm Muted Taupe (`#6E675F`), padding 6px 14px, radius 6px.
- **Active Filter:** Background Botanical Sage (`#536350`), border 1px solid `#536350`, text `#FDFBF7`.

### Checkboxes & Ritual Toggles
- Custom square box with 4px radius, 1px border in `#6E675F`. When checked, smoothly fills with Botanical Sage (`#536350`) featuring a hand-drawn-style ivory check glyph. Never use standard operating-system blue checkboxes.

### Audio Player & Mindful Dock
- Persistent floating pill docked 24px above mobile or desktop bottom screen margin.
- Styled in Frosted Linen (`rgba(250, 248, 244, 0.92)`), 1px border `#E5DFD5`, 16px radius, ambient diffuse shadow. Houses subtle pulsing progress waveform in Warm Clay Gold (`#BFA37C`) and minimal haptic play/pause controls.
